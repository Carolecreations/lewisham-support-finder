Essential numbers

  • 999Emergency (police, fire, ambulance)
  • 111NHS urgent medical advice. Press 2 for a mental health crisis
  • 116 123Samaritans, free, 24/7, if you need to talk
  • 0808 2000 247National Domestic Abuse Helpline, free, 24/7
  • 101Police, non-emergency
  • 020 8314 6000Lewisham Council main number (out of hours: emergencies only)
  • 0330 058 3464Community Connections Lewisham (food, local support)
  • 0800 231 5453Advice Lewisham (free advice: benefits, debt, housing)
  • 0808 800 4444Shelter housing helpline (homeless or at risk)

Calls to 0800 and 0808 numbers are free from UK landlines and mobiles.
